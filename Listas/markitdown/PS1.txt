liability in the balance sheet. So income taxes recorded on the income statement = taxes
paid (cash) + changes in deferred taxes. In real world applications, should we adjust the
textbook FCF formula, F CFt = EBITt(1 − τc) + Depreciation − CAP EXt − ∆N W Ct,
