# Practice plan — Class 1: What is corporate finance? Introduction and stylized facts

20 exercises from the course books, **in increasing order of complexity**. References only:
open the book at the page given. Notes: [[Aula/aula-01-notes|Class 1 notes]] · Rules:
[[rules/01_introducao_fatos|Topic 1]] · Index: [[Map/exercises-index|exercise index]].

Pages are **printed** pages (PDF = printed + 13 Tirole, + 18 Amaro de Matos, + 21 Higgins,
+ 24 Ross). Ross is the local *Fundamentos* 9th ed. and Higgins the local 13th ed. (not the
syllabus editions; see [[Livros/BOOKS|BOOKS]]). Every exercise below is `Verified = yes` in the
index, and every Tirole statement was re-read in the source for this plan.

## Weighting

Tirole's Introduction, Ch. 1 and Ch. 2 have no end-of-chapter exercises, so the hard end of
the list comes from Ch. 3 exercises on the §3.2 fixed-investment model, which class 1 previews.

| Subtopic | Weight | Exercises | Why this weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-investment model, credit rationing (Tirole §3.2) | 40% | 8 | the only mathematized model in the class; carries over to Classes 6–8 and exam questions |
| Governance and agency (Tirole Ch. 1; Ross 1.3–1.4) | 25% | 5 | the course's definition of the field; conceptual exam items |
| MM benchmark (Tirole §2.2) | 20% | 4 | the frictionless benchmark every later class relaxes |
| Stylized facts on financing (Tirole §2.5; Higgins §4.6) | 15% | 3 | descriptive; few existing exercises |

Levels: **Foundations** 8 · **Exam** 10 · **Advanced** 2.
