# Course program — Finanças II – Corporativas

Tier-2 file, loaded on demand. Source: `Finanças II.pdf` (Insper ementário, curriculum 202431,
período letivo 2024/34, 30 h).

## Ementa (summary)
Main topics in corporate finance and corporate governance, plus recent research. Taught from
practical problems through theoretical and empirical economic models of security design,
capital structure, governance and executive compensation.

## Bibliography

**Básica**
- Tirole, Jean. *The Theory of Corporate Finance*, 1st ed., Princeton University Press, 2006.
  Local copy is the 2005 Princeton printing (same 1st edition).

**Complementar**
- Amaro de Matos, João. *Theoretical Foundations of Corporate Finance*, 1st ed., Princeton UP, 2001.
  Local copy: same edition (© 2001, later printing). 101 exercises, solutions in Appendix C.
- Ross, S. A. et al. *Administração Financeira*, 10th ed., AMGH, 2015 (Ross, Westerfield, Jaffe & Lamb).
  ⚠️ Local copy is a **different book**: Ross, Westerfield, Jordan & Lamb, *Fundamentos de
  Administração Financeira*, 9th ed., AMGH/Bookman, 2013. Cite it as *Fundamentos*.
- Tirole (repeated from básica).
- Higgins, R. *Analysis for Financial Management*, McGraw-Hill, 2011 (edition number blank in the syllabus; 2011 printing is the 10th ed.).
  ⚠️ Local copy is Koski, Higgins & Mitton, **13th ed.** (© 2023); chapters and pages differ from 2011.

Usage: Tirole for every model and proof; Amaro de Matos for the formal MM/asymmetric-info
treatment; Ross *Fundamentos* and Higgins for valuation and practitioner computations.
Macro implications has no complementary source; Activism is only lightly covered outside Tirole.
