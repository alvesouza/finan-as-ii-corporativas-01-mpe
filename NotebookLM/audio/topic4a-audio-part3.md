Name and correct four misconceptions, each with its mechanism: a borrower without credit must be a bad risk (here she is creditworthy but cannot commit, and refusal for lack of collateral is not rationing in Baltensperger's sense); lenders could fix rationing by charging more (a higher repayment cuts the borrower's stake and causes misbehaviour); debt is always the best contract (only under risk neutrality and ignoring distress, overhang and asset substitution); a correlation between cash flow and investment proves financing constraints (cash flow also signals investment opportunities, hence the push for exogenous shocks such as De Mel et al.'s randomised grants).
