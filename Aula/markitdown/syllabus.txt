Course Syllabus: Finance II (Corporate Finance)

Availability: This course is available on the executive master’s in Economics

Instructor: Bernardo Ricca

Email: BernardoOGR@insper.edu.br

Teaching assistant: Lucas Negreiros

Email: lucasno1@al.insper.edu.br

Summary: This is a course in corporate ﬁnance and ﬁnancial intermediation. The course covers both key
aspects of the theory and empirics of corporate ﬁnance.

Objectives: The main goal of the course is to enable students to develop a solid knowledge of theoretical
corporate ﬁnance, get acquainted with recent empirical research in the ﬁeld, and make the correct use of the
tools in their professional lives.

Course requirements: Students are required to submit solutions to the problem sets and present a research
paper. Assignments may be completed in pairs.

Grading policy: Problem sets (10%), presentation of a research paper (10%), midterm exam (20%), ﬁnal
exam (60%).
