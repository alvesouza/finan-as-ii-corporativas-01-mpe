Bankruptcy costs

       More leverage increases the risk of ﬁnancial distress and bankruptcy
       A bankruptcy process transfers ownership to debtholders
       This transfer of control is not a violation of MM per se
       The violation comes from bankruptcy costs:

              Direct costs
              Indirect costs
       Such bankruptcy costs may countervail the tax advantage of debt
       ﬁnancing
