## Notes

- **Tirole edition.** The local PDF is the 2005 Princeton printing of the 1st edition; the
  syllabus cites 2006. Same edition, same pagination. Printed page = PDF page − 13.
- **Alternate Tirole copy.** `D:\Livros\Mercado\Economia\corporate-finance\top10\Jean Tirole-The Theory of Corporate Finance   -Princeton University Press (2009).epub` — not copied (the PDF keeps page numbers).
- **Ross et al.** The syllabus book is the Portuguese translation of Ross, Westerfield &
  Jaffe, *Corporate Finance* (the graduate-leaning text). The local *Fundamentos* is the
  Ross–Westerfield–Jordan introductory text: same authors' approach and overlapping
  valuation/capital-structure coverage, but different chapters and pages. Cite it as
  *Fundamentos*, never as *Administração Financeira*.
- **Higgins.** The 2011 printing is the 10th edition; the local 2022 copy (with Koski and
  Mitton) is newer, so chapter and page numbers may differ from the syllabus edition.
- All three new books were converted with the same column-aware extractor as Tirole;
  each `.md` marks pages with `<!-- page N -->` (PDF page).
