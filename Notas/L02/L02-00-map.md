# L02 — Topic 2A · Valuation, the NPV rule and the frictionless benchmark · Map

> **PDF.** [L02-notes.pdf](L02-notes.pdf) — this whole unit in one printable file (rendered from these notes; rebuild with `python -I ~/.claude/commands/templates/notes_pdf.py Notas/L02` after any edit).

> **One claim.** **Value is a present value at a risk-adjusted rate:** discount the unlevered free
> cash flows at the return that the assets' systematic risk commands. That rate comes from
> comparables only after **unlevering**, because $A=D+E$ makes asset returns and betas
> value-weighted averages of debt's and equity's. **The NPV rule is the shareholders' rule** when
> markets are frictionless. **In that benchmark financing is irrelevant:** with complete markets
> every claim is priced state by state, so slicing the same payoff into debt and equity cannot
> change its total value (MM I). The cost of equity rises exactly enough to keep the WACC fixed
> (MM II).

## The one result to hold

$$\boxed{\;A=\sum_s p_s\,X_s=\underbrace{\sum_s p_s\min(X_s,F)}_{D}+\underbrace{\sum_s p_s\max(X_s-F,0)}_{E}\quad\text{for every }F
\;\Longrightarrow\; r_E=r_A+\frac{D}{E}(r_A-r_D)\;}$$

> **Pricing is linear in payoffs and the two claims add up to the asset**: that is MM I. MM II is
> the same statement in returns.

## Scope and ceiling

- **Examinable:** the slides' content (C layer) above, at the slides' level: one-period, finitely
  many states, CAPM rates, no taxes.
- **Named only (G):** Fisher separation, Farkas/positive state prices, incomplete markets'
  non-unique prices, MM with state prices (Amaro de Matos); Tirole's risk-neutral proof and
  payout irrelevance.
- **Out of this unit:** taxes, distress costs and the trade-off (Topic 2B, [[../L03/L03-00-map|L03]]);
  debt overhang, risk-shifting and asymmetric information are flagged where the slides flag them
  and left to the agency and information topics of the syllabus.
