Model. Holmström–Tirole fixed-investment model as in Tirole, The Theory of Corporate Finance (Princeton, 2006), §3.2.1–3.2.2, eqs. (3.1)–(3.6), printed pp. 115–118. Risk neutrality, zero interest rate, limited liability, competitive lenders. Every curve is the closed-form expression; nothing is fitted or solved numerically.

Defaults. Class 1 calibration, checked in Videos/aula-01-what-is-corporate-finance/check_numbers.py.

Edge cases. If pL = pH and B > 0, no stake can induce effort, so Ā is infinite. If (3.1) fails, the project is not worth funding at any A. If (3.2) fails, a loan that tolerates shirking could also be granted, and (3.3) is no longer the whole funding condition; the page still shows (3.3) and flags it.
