# Class Plan and Study Guide — 16 classes

**Status: proposal.** Built from the syllabus content list (`Finanças II.pdf`), not from the
professor's calendar. The course is 30 h, so 16 classes ≈ 1 h 50 min each. Replace class
contents with the real slides as they arrive in `Aula/` (via `/intake`).

Pages are printed pages. Exercise IDs are from [[Map/exercises-index|exercises-index]]:
Tirole `C.N`; Amaro de Matos `subsection #n`; Higgins `C.N` (odd ones have answers);
Ross *Fundamentos* `C.N`. Ross and Higgins are not the syllabus editions ([[Livros/BOOKS|BOOKS]]).

## Class 1 — What is corporate finance? Introduction and stylized facts
- **Before:** Tirole Introduction (pp. 1–12); Ch. 1 §1.1–1.2 (pp. 15–28)
- **After:** Tirole Ch. 2 §2.3–2.5 (debt and equity instruments, financing patterns, pp. 80–102)
- **Practise:** none in Tirole (descriptive). Ross CQ 1.x conceptual questions
- **Rules:** [[rules/01_introducao_fatos|Topic 1]]

## Class 2 — Valuation I: NPV and cost of capital
- **Before:** Ross Cap. 5–6 (time value, DCF), Cap. 9 (NPV and other criteria)
- **After:** Ross 13.7 (CAPM), 14.4 (CMPC/WACC); Higgins Ch. 7, Ch. 8 (WACC, beta)
- **Practise:** Ross 9.1–9.10, 14.1–14.10; Higgins 7.1, 7.3, 7.5, 8.1, 8.3 (answers in book)
- **Rules:** [[rules/02_valuation|Topic 2]]

## Class 3 — Valuation II: valuing the firm; real options
- **Before:** Higgins Ch. 9 (free cash flow DCF, terminal value, comparables)
- **After:** Higgins Ch. 8 real options; Amaro de Matos Ch. 1 (Fisher separation, Prop. 1.1.1 p. 12; state prices; binomial real options)
- **Practise:** Higgins 9.1, 9.3, 9.5; Amaro de Matos 1.1.8 #8 (prove the NPV rule), 1.2.6 #5, 1.3.4 #4 (real options)
- **Rules:** [[rules/02_valuation|Topic 2]]

## Class 4 — The frictionless benchmark: Modigliani–Miller
- **Before:** Tirole §2.2 (pp. 77–80); Amaro de Matos §2.1 (MM1 p. 42, MM2 p. 43)
- **After:** Amaro de Matos §4.1 (MM3, dividend irrelevance, p. 100); Ross 16.2–16.4; Higgins Ch. 6 appendix (pp. 223–227)
- **Practise:** Amaro de Matos 2.1.5 #2 (home-made leverage), 2.1.5 #3, 2.1.5 #4, 4.1.7 #5
- **Rules:** [[rules/03_benchmark_mm|Topic 3]]

## Class 5 — Taxes and default costs: the trade-off
- **Before:** Amaro de Matos §2.2 (taxes, Miller 1977, DeAngelo–Masulis)
- **After:** Ross 16.5–16.6, 16.10 (bankruptcy costs, static trade-off); Higgins Ch. 6 (tax benefits, distress costs p. 212)
- **Practise:** Amaro de Matos 2.1.5 #1, 2.2.7 #1, 2.2.7 #2; Ross 16.1–16.10
- **Rules:** [[rules/03_benchmark_mm|Topic 3]]
- **Bridge:** MM breaks down → why agency frictions matter (Class 6)

## Class 6 — Agency I: moral hazard and credit rationing
- **Before:** Tirole §3.1–3.2 (pp. 113–125) — the fixed-investment model
- **After:** Tirole §3.6 (verifiable income), §3.8 (nonverifiable income)
- **Practise:** Tirole 3.1, 3.2, 3.3 (answers in Part VII)
- **Rules:** [[rules/04_agencia_estrutura_governanca|Topic 4]]

## Class 7 — Agency II: debt overhang and borrowing capacity
- **Before:** Tirole §3.3–3.4 (pp. 125–130); §4.1–4.3 (pp. 157–171)
- **After:** Tirole §4.4–4.5; Amaro de Matos §3.1 (agency costs of equity and debt)
- **Practise:** Tirole 3.6, 3.8, 4.1, 4.3; Amaro de Matos 3.1.5 #9 (Myers underinvestment)
- **Rules:** [[rules/04_agencia_estrutura_governanca|Topic 4]]

## Class 8 — Agency III: control rights and corporate governance
- **Before:** Tirole §1.3 (boards), §1.6 (debt as governance); §10.2 (pp. 389–398)
- **After:** Tirole §10.3–10.4; Amaro de Matos §5.1 (Aghion–Bolton)
- **Practise:** Tirole 10.1, 10.2, 10.7; Amaro de Matos 5.1.6 #3, 5.1.6 #4
- **Rules:** [[rules/04_agencia_estrutura_governanca|Topic 4]]

## Class 9 — Liquidity I: maturity and the liquidity–scale tradeoff
- **Before:** Tirole §5.1–5.3 (pp. 199–213)
- **After:** Ross Cap. 18 (short-term financing, credit lines), Cap. 19 (cash management)
- **Practise:** Tirole 5.1, 5.2, 5.5; Ross 19.1–19.5
- **Rules:** [[rules/05_liquidez|Topic 5]]

## Class 10 — Liquidity II: risk management and free cash flow
- **Before:** Tirole §5.4–5.6 (pp. 213–228)
- **After:** Amaro de Matos §5.2 (Diamond 1991 maturity and liquidation risk)
- **Practise:** Tirole 5.3, 5.6, 5.10; Amaro de Matos 5.2.5 #1, 5.2.5 #5
- **Rules:** [[rules/05_liquidez|Topic 5]]

## Class 11 — Asymmetric information I: lemons and the pecking order
- **Before:** Tirole §6.1–6.2 (pp. 237–249)
- **After:** Amaro de Matos §3.2.3 (Myers–Majluf); Higgins Ch. 6 "Market Signaling" (p. 216), pecking order (p. 218); Ross 16.8
- **Practise:** Tirole 6.1, 6.2, 6.5; Amaro de Matos 3.2.6 #4
- **Rules:** [[rules/06_assimetria_informacao|Topic 6]]

## Class 12 — Asymmetric information II: signalling and IPOs
- **Before:** Tirole §6.3 (pp. 249–264)
- **After:** Amaro de Matos §3.2 (Ross 1977, Leland–Pyle p. 84), §4.2 (dividend signalling), Ch. 6 (IPO underpricing); Ross 15.5–15.6, 17.5
- **Practise:** Tirole 6.8, 6.10; Amaro de Matos 3.2.6 #1, 4.2.5 #1, 6.2.7 #1, 6.2.7 #5
- **Rules:** [[rules/06_assimetria_informacao|Topic 6]]

## Class 13 — Activism and monitoring
- **Before:** Tirole §1.4 (pp. 36–43); Ch. 8 §8.1–8.3 (pp. 333–350)
- **After:** Tirole Ch. 9 §9.2–9.3 (pp. 356–369); Amaro de Matos §8.1.2 (large shareholder), §8.2.4 (proxy fights)
- **Practise:** Tirole 8.2, 9.1, 9.6; Amaro de Matos 8.1.5 #5, 8.2.6 #10
- **Rules:** [[rules/07_ativismo|Topic 7]]

## Class 14 — Mergers and acquisitions: takeovers
- **Before:** Tirole §1.5 (pp. 43–51); Ch. 11 (pp. 425–441)
- **After:** Amaro de Matos Ch. 8 §8.1 (Grossman–Hart, Shleifer–Vishny), Ch. 7 (LBOs); Higgins Ch. 9 pp. 354–367
- **Practise:** Tirole 11.1; Amaro de Matos 8.1.5 #1, 8.1.5 #9, 7.2.4 #3, 8.2.6 #3
- **Rules:** [[rules/08_fusoes_aquisicoes|Topic 8]]

## Class 15 — Asset values: from M&A to the macroeconomy
- **Before:** Tirole Ch. 14 §14.2 Shleifer–Vishny (pp. 499–509)
- **After:** Tirole §14.3 Kiyotaki–Moore (pp. 509–515)
- **Practise:** Tirole 14.2, 14.3, 14.4
- **Rules:** [[rules/08_fusoes_aquisicoes|Topic 8]] · [[rules/09_implicacoes_macro|Topic 9]]

## Class 16 — Macroeconomic implications
- **Before:** Tirole Ch. 13 §13.2–13.3 (pp. 471–484)
- **After:** Tirole §13.4 (financial accelerator); Ch. 15 (aggregate liquidity); Ch. 16 optional
